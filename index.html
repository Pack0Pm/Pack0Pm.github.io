Subtitle Creator • Vocaloid / UTAU / Synthesizer V

Hi! I'm Packpm 👋

I'm a subtitle creator from Thailand who mainly works with Vocaloid, UTAU, and Synthesizer V music.

🎬 I create Thai subtitles and occasionally English subtitles for music videos I enjoy.

Aegisub YTSubConverter GitHub

My Goal 🌱

20+ / 100 MVs

A small record of my journey toward subtitling 100 music videos.

I started making subtitles simply because I wanted more Vocaloid songs to be accessible to Thai-speaking listeners. Over time, it became something I genuinely love doing.

I also sometimes work directly with producers to provide official subtitles for their MVs.
